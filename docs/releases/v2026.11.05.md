## Changed

- **Places are called by their common names.** Ultimate Places now names a place after its English Wikipedia article: Sagrada Família instead of "Basilica and Expiatory Church of the Holy Family", Mount Paektu, Pico de Orizaba, Iguazu Falls, Kangchenjunga, Tian Shan and Lake Titicaca. 35 places are renamed; their review history is kept.

## Fixed

- **The Wikipedia links in Ultimate Iran no longer open Dutch Wikipedia.** The two country decks share their note types, so the last deck imported set the language for both. Each card now takes its language from its own country.
- **A Wikipedia link only appears when the article exists.** Places without an English article (Texel, the Heritage district of Yazd) get no English link, and the Persian or Dutch link on a country deck place is now taken from the article Wikidata lists, so the three Iranian places without a Persian article (the Heritage district of Yazd, the Hyrcanian Forests, the Niavaran Complex) no longer link to an error page.
- Ultimate Countries is the same as the previous release.
