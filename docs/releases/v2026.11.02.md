## New

- **Better river maps in Ultimate Places.** The 28 river cards now show where the river begins and ends, which way it flows and the best-known places on the way, all named on the map except the river itself.
  - **Source:** mountains for a range, plateau or pass, an icy peak for a glacier, a rock with water coming out for a spring, trees or reeds for a swamp or wetland, and a small group of buildings for a city, town, commune or village. A lake or reservoir is shown as a lake. A source that is itself a river (the Breg and Brigach for the Danube, the Marañón and Ucayali for the Amazon) is drawn in grey with its name.
  - **Mouth:** the sea, lake or river it flows into is named. Rivers that end inland fade out or run into a drawn lake, delta or dried sea ("Aral Sea (dried)").
  - **On the way:** Victoria Falls, the Aswan and Three Gorges dams, the Grand Canyon and lakes such as Lake Geneva when they lie on the river.
  - **Arrows** along the river show the direction of flow.
- **Texel** joins Ultimate Netherlands Geography, and the **Hyrcanian Forests** and the **Heritage district of Yazd** join Ultimate Iran Geography. Their names are spoken like the others.

## Changed

- River maps are zoomed in more tightly than the other place maps.
- The length of five features (Atrek, the Appalachian Mountains, the Eastern Ghats, the Great Rift Valley and Sikhote-Alin) is now left empty because Wikidata and Wikipedia disagree about it.
- The native name of De Kuip is spelled with a plain "ij" ("Stadion Feijenoord").
- Ultimate Countries is the same as the previous release.
