## New

- **Place maps show what lies around the place.** In all three decks with places:
  - **Lakes** show the river that flows out and the best-known rivers that flow in, up to four in all (the White Nile, Kagera and Mara around Lake Victoria; the Volga, Ural and Kura around the Caspian).
  - **Canals** name the seas, lakes and rivers they join, inside the water at each end: the Mediterranean and the Red Sea for the Suez Canal, the Atlantic and the Pacific for the Panama Canal, the North Sea and the Baltic for the Kiel Canal.
  - **Waterfalls and canyons** show the river they lie on, with a waterfall or gorge picture: the Zambezi at Victoria Falls, the Rhine at the Rhine Falls, the Colorado at the Grand Canyon.
  - **Mountains and volcanoes** show their range, shaded with its name: the Himalayas around Everest, the Alps around Mont Blanc.
  - **Mountain ranges, plateaus, deserts, peninsulas and basins** show their highest point: Mont Blanc in the Alps, Aconcagua in the Andes, Emi Koussi in the Sahara.
  - **Peninsulas, isthmuses, islands and reefs** name the seas around them: the Red Sea, Persian Gulf and Arabian Sea around Arabia, the North Sea and Wadden Sea around Terschelling.
  - **Seas** name the seas and oceans they connect to.
  - **Deltas, basins, valleys, plains and wetlands** show the rivers crossing them: the Syr Darya through the Ferghana Valley, the Amazon through its basin. Deserts and wetlands add dune or reed pictures.
  - **Landmarks** show the river they stand on (the Yamuna at the Taj Mahal), and **national parks** their nearest town (Ede for the Hoge Veluwe).
  - **Polders** name the lakes and seas they border.
  - Rivers on these maps are thin and blue, without flow arrows, so the cards don't look like river cards.
- **Links on every answer.** The back of every card in all four decks ends with small links to the Wikipedia article, Google Images, YouTube and Google Maps; Ultimate Iran and Ultimate Netherlands add the Persian or Dutch article.
- **Tap a map to zoom in on Ultimate Places too**, as in the other decks.
- **Every map is checked as it is drawn.** No name may overlap another, be cut off or cover the inset, and the place must be big enough to see or have a magnifier.

## Changed

- **A sea a river flows into is named inside that sea.** The Bohai Sea on the Yellow River was printed in the Yellow Sea, and the Caspian on the Volga in the Black Sea. When the sea is too narrow for its name, the name sits at the river's mouth.
- Ultimate Countries only gains the links.
