## New

- **Lakes, canals, waterfalls and canyons show what they connect to.** In all three decks with places:
  - **Lakes** show the river that flows out and the best-known rivers that flow in, drawn in grey and named, up to four in all (the White Nile, Kagera and Mara around Lake Victoria; the Volga, Ural and Kura around the Caspian).
  - **Canals** name the seas, lakes and rivers they join, inside the water at each end: the Mediterranean and the Red Sea for the Suez Canal, the Atlantic and the Pacific for the Panama Canal, the North Sea and the Baltic for the Kiel Canal.
  - **Waterfalls and canyons** show the river they lie on, with a waterfall or gorge picture: the Zambezi at Victoria Falls, the Rhine at the Rhine Falls, the Colorado at the Grand Canyon.
  - These rivers are thin and grey, without flow arrows, so the cards don't look like river cards.
- **Every map is checked as it is drawn.** No name may overlap another, be cut off or cover the inset, and the place must be big enough to see or have a magnifier.
