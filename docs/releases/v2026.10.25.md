## Fixed

- **City answers now name the city on the map.** Many city answer maps in the country decks were identical to the question; the answer city is now always labelled.
- **Hints no longer give the name away** in the country decks: short names, native names, aliases and translations are masked, and the Persian Gulf is never called anything else.
- **Places outside the country are gone** from the country decks (Little Ararat, the Armenian Highlands, rivers that only touch Iran), and landmarks no longer name a city in a neighbouring country.
- **Rivers are drawn whole**, for example the Waal.
- **Sea neighbours fit their map**: Oman, Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Azerbaijan are no longer cut off.
- **UNESCO sites are tagged consistently**, including the parts of an inscribed site, and natural sites such as the Lut Desert have their natural kind. Schiphol is back.
- **Provinces named like their centre** no longer get the two trivial centre cards.
- **Persian names keep their half-spaces**, for example فرودگاه بین‌المللی امام خمینی.
- **Place cards in the country decks show the answer map with the place named** on the back.
- **Persian Gulf description:** Ultimate Places no longer mentions another name for it.

## Changed

- The place note type of the country decks has a new field, so Anki asks to update the note type when you import over version 2026.10.24.1.
