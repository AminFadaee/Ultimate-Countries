## Changed

- **Better maps for thirty-five small island countries and territories** in Ultimate Countries. Each now has a red circle around its islands, and most add a magnifier that shows them larger. Scattered groups get one magnifier per island group: the Caribbean Netherlands has three (Bonaire, Saba and Sint Eustatius), Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha have three, French Polynesia has three, and Pitcairn, Wallis and Futuna, the Northern Mariana Islands and the Cook Islands have two. Most also zoom out to show a larger neighbour so you can see where the islands are: Australia for Tuvalu, Tonga, Samoa and American Samoa, India for the Maldives, Madagascar for the Comoros, Papua New Guinea for the Marshall Islands and Micronesia, Chile for Pitcairn, New Zealand for the Cook Islands and Tanzania for Seychelles.
- The countries and territories covered are: American Samoa, Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, Åland, Bahrain, the British Virgin Islands, Cabo Verde, the Cayman Islands, the Caribbean Netherlands, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, the Comoros, the Cook Islands, the Faroe Islands, French Polynesia, Grenada, Guadeloupe, Kiribati, the Maldives, the Marshall Islands, Mauritius, Micronesia, the Northern Mariana Islands, Palau, Pitcairn, Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Samoa, São Tomé and Príncipe, Seychelles, Tokelau, Tonga, Turks and Caicos, Tuvalu, the US Virgin Islands and Wallis and Futuna.
- Tiny islets are drawn at their real relative size in the magnifier, so a speck such as Redonda does not look like a third island next to Antigua and Barbuda.
- All other maps are unchanged.

The Ultimate Places, Ultimate Iran Geography and Ultimate Netherlands Geography decks are the same as the previous release.
