## Changed

- **Better maps for ten small island countries** in Ultimate Countries: Antigua and Barbuda, Bahrain, Cabo Verde, the Comoros, Grenada, the Maldives, Mauritius, Palau, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines and Seychelles. Each now has a red circle around its islands and most add a magnifier showing them larger. The Maldives, Cabo Verde and Seychelles keep the circle without the magnifier. The Comoros show Madagascar, the Maldives show India, Palau and Saint Vincent are zoomed out, and Seychelles shows the East African coast. Tiny islets are drawn at their real relative size, so a speck such as Redonda does not look like a third island next to Antigua and Barbuda.
- All other maps are unchanged.

The Ultimate Places, Ultimate Iran Geography and Ultimate Netherlands Geography decks are the same as the previous release.
