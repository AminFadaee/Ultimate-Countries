## New

- **City maps name the neighbouring countries**, like an atlas page, so a city in a small country shows where it is. The country itself is never named.
- **The photo of a place now sits inside its map's magnifier**, in place of the dot. A map with no magnifier, such as the Great Wall, shows the photo in the corner.
- **The Great Wall and Hadrian's Wall are drawn as lines** on their maps, taken from OpenStreetMap.
- **New regions:** Micronesia, Polynesia and the Malay Archipelago.
- **Sharper maps:** every map is now saved at 2000 px wide, twice the previous width, so zooming in stays crisp. The decks are larger as a result (about 57 MB and 66 MB).

## Fixed

- **Siberia, the Sahel and Melanesia had wrong maps.** Siberia now follows its real provinces, the Sahel is its proper belt across Africa, and Melanesia includes Indonesian New Guinea and the whole island group.
- **Duplicate landmark cards:** where Wikidata says one landmark is part of another, only the better-known one is kept. The Giza pyramid complex, Angkor and the Acropolis of Athens are gone; the Great Pyramid, Angkor Wat and the Parthenon remain.
- **Micronesia, Polynesia and Melanesia list their countries correctly**, including territories that lie mostly inside them.
- **Region maps for Scandinavia, Central America and the Indian subcontinent** keep their country-based shapes.

Remove the three deleted landmark notes by hand in your collection, because Anki never deletes notes on import.
