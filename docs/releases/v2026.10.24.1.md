## New

- **Country decks.** A deck of your own country, in the same style as Ultimate Countries and Ultimate Places. The first two are **Ultimate Iran Geography** and **Ultimate Netherlands Geography**:
  - **Provinces:** each province on a map with its centre as a black dot, the centre by name, and the province from its centre.
  - **Cities:** every provincial centre and the cities that matter, on maps that name the other cities and the neighbouring countries.
  - **Neighbours:** every land neighbour and every country across the sea, with the capital, flag and facts of each.
  - **Places:** mountains, rivers, lakes, islands, seas, parks, UNESCO sites and the landmarks the country is known for, with a photo card where there is a photo.
  - **Native names:** every name is also shown in the country's own language, for example Mashhad in Persian or Noord-Holland in Dutch.
- **Disputed areas** appear in the disputed colour on the country decks, and the islands of the Persian Gulf are included.
- **Better photos.** Photos on the Places and country decks get an automatic tone correction when a deck is built: contrast is stretched only on flat images, brightness is adjusted only on very dark or washed-out ones, and a light sharpening is applied. Photos that are already good are barely touched.
- Tall photos are no longer cropped on the cards.
- Photo cards on the country decks are limited to places whose shape identifies them; the others show a description with the name blanked out as a hint.
- Build a deck for any other country with `geography country CODE` and `geography country-deck CODE` (see the README).
