## Changed

- **Better maps for seventeen small island countries** in Ultimate Countries: Antigua and Barbuda, Bahrain, Cabo Verde, the Comoros, Grenada, Kiribati, the Maldives, the Marshall Islands, Mauritius, Micronesia, Palau, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Samoa, São Tomé and Príncipe, Seychelles, Tonga and Tuvalu. Each now has a red circle around its islands. Some add a magnifier, and most zoom out to show a larger neighbour so you can see where the islands are: Australia for Tuvalu, Tonga and Samoa, India for the Maldives, Madagascar for the Comoros, Papua New Guinea for the Marshall Islands and Micronesia, Tanzania for Seychelles. Tiny islets are drawn at their real relative size, so a speck such as Redonda does not look like a third island next to Antigua and Barbuda.
- All other maps are unchanged.

The Ultimate Places, Ultimate Iran Geography and Ultimate Netherlands Geography decks are the same as the previous release.
