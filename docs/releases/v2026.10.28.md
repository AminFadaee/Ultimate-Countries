## New

- **Spoken names in the country decks.** A ▶ button next to the native name of every province, city and place speaks it: Dutch in Ultimate Netherlands Geography and Persian in Ultimate Iran Geography. Nothing plays by itself. The voices are synthetic: Supertonic-3 for Dutch and Piper for Persian.

## Fixed

- The country-deck descriptions now credit the Wikimedia Commons photos and no longer say "Netherlands's".

## Changed

- The province, city and place note types of the country decks have a new field for the audio button. When you import over an earlier version, tick **Merge notetypes** and update existing notes, or the button will not appear.
